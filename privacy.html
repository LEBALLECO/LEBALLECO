Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 25 Jan 2026

At LEBALLECO, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy outlines the types of personal information we collect and how we use, store, and protect that information. By using our website and services, you agree to the terms of this policy.

We collect information from you when you use our website, contact us, or engage with our services. This information may include:

  • Personal Identification Information (Name, email address, phone number, etc.)

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including:

  • To provide and improve our services: We use your information to respond to your inquiries, provide services, and maintain communication regarding your projects.
  • To personalize your experience: Your information helps us tailor our content and services to better suit your needs.

  • For website analytics: We use non-personal information to understand how visitors use our website, improve the site’s functionality, and enhance the user experience.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal data, including:

  • Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption to protect sensitive information transmitted through our website.
  • Regular security audits to ensure the integrity of our systems.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of data transmission over the internet is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
